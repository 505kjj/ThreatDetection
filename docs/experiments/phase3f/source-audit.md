# Phase 3F source audit

Sources checked 24 November 2026 unless the attempt is explicitly attributed to Phase 3E. “Available” refers to the published data interface. Only rows admitted by the frozen protocol appear in the evaluation. A source license does not certify a label or a date, and a public webpage does not imply permission to redistribute a dataset.

## Threat intelligence

| Source and available access | Rights and commercial research | Evidence fields and decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PhishTank Cisco Talos developer download | The terms distinguish site content from data and explicitly permit commercial use of API data. The published schema includes full URL, verified status, submission and verification timestamps, and a target field when reported. The public feed is a rolling set of verified URLs reported online. [Terms](https://phishtank.org/terms.php), [developer schema](https://phishtank.net/developer_info.php) | Phase 3E received HTTP 429 from the production hostname and zero records from development downloads. Phase 3F used a separately published immutable historical research archive. No retry storm or alternate access to the rate-limited feed. Feed target labels and campaign IDs cannot be assumed complete. |
| Behind the Bait, public Zenodo archive | Yasin, Fatima, Khan and Afzal. The Zenodo record is CC BY 4.0 and the underlying published PhishTank data terms permit commercial use. Accepted workbook downloaded through Zenodo’s public file API. Only the listed workbook cells were parsed as metadata. [Zenodo record](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.8371046), [article](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dib.2023.109959) | Both downloads matched publisher size and MD5. Workbook 20 provided a full original URL, PhishTank record ID, dated submission and explicit verified status. Workbook 1 lacked a row-level valid-phish column and was excluded. Neither supplies independently verified brand, geography or campaign identity. Verified status is historical and source-reported. No separate verification timestamp in workbook 20. Source lineage may overlap prior corpora even after domain exclusion. |
| OpenPhish public community feed and historical database | Historical data with richer metadata has separate access and permitted-use terms. The community URL feed and an academic program do not grant this project a dated commercial research export. [Historical product](https://www.openphish.com/phishing_database.html), [terms](https://www.openphish.com/terms.html) | No permitted archive was available. Do not infer original observation dates from a downloaded URL list. Target and campaign evidence were not acquired. |
| CERT Polska Warning List | Official service permits Warning List processing without special permission. Version 2 is the current public distribution interface and retains recent records. [Specification](https://github.com/CERT-Polska/phishing-api/blob/master/SPECIFICATION.md), [version 2 notice](https://cert.pl/en/posts/2025/01/hole-v1-deprecation-notice/) | The feed is a domain warning list for scams affecting Polish users. A domain and addition timestamp cannot establish the exact full observed URL, verified financial target, or campaign. We did not build artificial root URLs from it. |
| URLhaus | Public malware-URL API documents an Auth-Key and fair-use conditions. Commercial needs may require a paid service. [Community API and terms](https://urlhaus.abuse.ch/api/) | A malware delivery designation, added date and exact URL cannot establish a credential phishing label or banking target. No eligible financial research feed was acquired. |
| APWG eCrime Exchange | Member data sharing agreement or research program approval for qualifying institutions. Published API documentation is public, but feed access and redistribution depend on approval and contract. [Access and controls](https://apwg.org/ecx-frameworks-conventions-and-controls), [research grant](https://www.antiphishing.org/membership/research) | Promising source of verified phishing submissions and campaign data under permission. There was no agreement or credential for this work. A same-lineage label source must never also be the sole reputation evidence for a replay. |

## Academic datasets

| Source and access | Rights and evidence | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PhishVN V5 | Open metadata CC BY 4.0, specifically distinguished from gated page captures. Reports full URL rows, provenance, time and confidence tiers. Its bank scenario is inferred from tokens, and named organization evidence is generally missing. The paper reports the national-feed core ends in February 2025. [Release](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/b97hxbxtpd/5), [article](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dib.2026.113195) | Phase 3E saw HTTP 403/502 and obtained no checksum-verified open archive. The authors’ public adjudication sheets lacked original row dates and mostly lacked absolute URLs. No rows used in Phase 3F. Vietnamese labels could inform separate transfer research, with upstream rights and provenance exclusions. |
| LegitPhish v2 | Mendeley lists CC BY 4.0. Study describes URLs, verification work and shared inputs including PhishTank and URLhaus. Publisher total and class counts differ by one. [Release](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/hx4m73v2sf/2), [article](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dib.2025.111972) | Original observation and verification timestamps, campaign identifiers, target provenance and source-level overlap are unresolved. Publisher license alone does not resolve upstream terms. No rows admitted. |
| CIC-Trap4Phish 2025 | Published university dataset covers URLs and several additional artifact types. UNB asks users to cite dataset use. [Dataset](https://www.unb.ca/cic/datasets/trap4phish2025.html), [dataset policy](https://www.unb.ca/cic/datasets/index.html) | Some data are malicious categories other than phishing. Exact URL evidence, original dates, brand/campaign adjudication and upstream overlap would need separate checks. No samples or executable content obtained. |
| PhreshPhish | Public pinned CC BY 4.0 release for anti-phishing use, with author commercial-use clarification. Contains full URL, label, collection date and target fields. Target labels and collection lineages can have their own limitations. [Pinned release](https://huggingface.co/datasets/phreshphish/phreshphish/blob/eca603e0ecd4f698f8d4d226c088659e72681239/README.md), [author clarification](https://huggingface.co/datasets/phreshphish/phreshphish/discussions/14) | Already used for Phase 3C and Phase 3D training and evaluation. These partitions were indexed to exclude overlap. They are not independent evidence for Phase 3F. |
| Phishing and Legitimate Websites V2 | Zenodo record flags CC BY 4.0 and advertises URLs with screenshots and features from PhishTank and Tranco. Public files are approximately 0.8 GB and 4.5 GB. [Release](https://zenodo.org/records/23477065) | The deposit does not establish per-record observation and verification timestamps, financial target or campaign quality, or rights of each upstream list. No large archive or screenshots were downloaded. |

## Indian financial sources

| Source and available access | What could be verified | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reserve Bank of India | Official public dated notices describe specific scams, including a 2018 central-bank impersonation. This notice identifies a bare address and publication date. It does not confirm scheme, exact URL path, original observation timestamp or rights for a bulk archive. [RBI notice](https://www.rbi.org.in/commonPerson/english/Scripts/PressReleases.aspx?Id=2628) | Useful evidence for scoping a future authority-verified dataset. No synthetic scheme or additional URL was created. |
| NPCI and UPI publications | First-party safety and payment documentation establishes intended legitimate destinations and describes fraud reports. Public guidance is not a verified malicious HTTP URL archive with per-record observation date and campaign identity. [NPCI UPI guidance](https://www.npci.org.in/what-we-do/upi/faqs) | The NPCI control was a previously observed domain and excluded. No UPI phishing label inferred from a payment keyword or handle. |
| I4C suspect repository | Public individual indicator lookup, without a documented bulk full-URL export, per-row evidence trail or commercial reuse agreement. A suspect report by itself is not adjudication. [I4C lookup](https://cybercrime.gov.in/Webform/suspect_search_repository.aspx) | No CAPTCHA or access restriction bypass and no dataset rows. |
| CERT-In and CSIRT-Fin | Financial-sector reports and advisories are publicly available. A published advisory identifies a fake UPI handle category, which is not an HTTP URL. No rights-cleared historical full-URL feed with verified per-record financial target and campaign was identified. [CERT-In publications](https://www.cert-in.org.in/s2cMainServlet?pageid=WHTPVIEW01), [UPI advisory](https://www.cert-in.org.in/s2cMainServlet?CACODE=CICA-2020-2711&pageid=PUBADV01) | Audited. No positive URL records admitted. |
| First-party banks and payment services | Published public login, verification, payment and security locators support factual internal numeric controls. We used the observation day of the official documentation or URL. No participant traffic, login, transaction or document content was collected. Source rights remain with each publisher. [PNB](https://www.pnb.bank.in/online-registartion-rbanking.html), [IDFC FIRST](https://www.idfcfirst.bank.in/new-domain), [Bank of Baroda](https://bankofbaroda.bank.in/digital-products/instant-banking/bob-world), [Canara](https://www.canarabank.bank.in/pages/Net-banking), [Union Bank](https://www.unionbankofindia.bank.in/en/details/internet-banking-overview), [Indian Bank](https://indianbank.bank.in/en/web/guest/internet-banking), [Bank of India](https://bankofindia.bank.in/safe-banking), [Federal Bank](https://www.federal.bank.in/federal-bank-website-moves-to-new-domain-under-rbi-compliance), [IndusInd](https://www.indusind.bank.in/in/en/personal/login.html), [Cashfree](https://www.cashfree.com/hosted-checkout/), [PhonePe](https://www.phonepe.com/business-solutions/) | 53 candidate controls, including 15 previously reviewed candidates from Phase 3E. Only the 29 surviving earlier domain and lexical-family exclusions were tested in Phase 3F. |
| Research partnerships or contracted historical feeds | The evidence still missing is a dated licensed export supplied by an authorized bank, PSP, CERT or provider. It needs full observed URLs, quality-reviewed financial target and campaign labels, source lineage, and written permission for internal commercial-product research and aggregate reporting. | No partner agreement or private feed was available. Any reputation snapshots must come from a distinct permitted lineage and have existed at the time of the evaluated observation. |
