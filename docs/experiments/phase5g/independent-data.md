[Prior source policy](../../../research/phase3e/protocol.json), [publisher schema](https://github.com/vuthainguyen1602/phishvn/blob/master/docs/schema.md)

Gold handling status and lexical brand tags alone do not certify credential phishing, the exact page, jurisdiction or an impersonated institution. The publisher's 200-row audit independently adjudicates a sample, with many ambiguous or broader abuse entries, and cannot confer adjudication on the release as a whole. Trust-registry sources are distinct from Tranco ranking negatives, but row-level date and page ownership evidence still matter. Do not discard a real observed scheme or fill in a missing scheme to match a model's inputs. [Publisher datasheet](https://github.com/vuthainguyen1602/phishvn/blob/master/docs/datasheet.md)

The release's internal domain split/duplicate table addresses overlap within PhishVN. External domain, URL, family and campaign overlap against this project remains unmeasured. An older historical cross-source study would need its own registered question, original observations with usable schemes, row-level adjudication, and new custody. Paired PhishVN captures are a separate gated release with research-only rights, and this project has no approved copy.
